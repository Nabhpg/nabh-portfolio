# Vision

## Site map
- `/` cinematic 3D scroll journey (the "trailer")
- `/work`, `/work/[slug]` calm case studies · `/writing` · `/about` · `/now` · `/admin` (private)

## Home page: a journey through Nabh's mind
One continuous dark, cinematic 3D world. Each skill = a chapter the camera travels through.
Scroll-DRIVEN (native scroll + pinned sections), never hard scroll-hijacking.

**Signature thread: the moon.** From Nabh's poem. Present in every chapter; phase follows scroll —
hilal (crescent) at the prologue → mehtab (full) at the epilogue.

| # | Chapter | Awe moment |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Prologue ✅ | A blade of light unveils a fountain pen in the dark; it signs Nabh's real autograph as you scroll. (Crescent moon to be added.) |
| 1 | Philosophy / Mind | Scattered particles merge into one glowing sphere (Advaita); Devanagari constellations. |
| 2 | Poetry | Dark ocean; Hindi/Urdu calligraphy draws itself stroke by stroke, rising like ink. |
| 3 | Film & Motion ✅ (plays right after the prologue) | Camera travels to a real scanned 8mm cine camera, half orbit, film strip unwinds toward the viewer; then editing + motion text staged like editing software. |
| 4 | Motion & Design | Floating glass panels driven by animated graph-editor bezier curves. |
| 5 | Growth / Markgenics | Particle funnel: reach → engagement → sale. |
| 6 | Building | Wireframes assemble into solid forms (Tattva, SATARK, apps, this site). |
| 7 | Epilogue / Contact | Full moon, fog clears, "Let's make something." |

Chapters are isolated scene modules; admin controls order, copy, featured work.

## Visual language (shared "camera")
Dark, film grain, haze/fog, bloom, vignette, slight chromatic aberration, light leaks, subtle glass UI.
Palette from PROFILE.md aesthetic.

## Non-negotiables
- Name + one-line identity visible <1s, before 3D loads. Nav always reachable. "Skip intro / Quick view".
- Real HTML text (selectable, SEO). 60fps target, adaptive quality, compressed assets, lazy scenes.
- Separate mobile choreography. `prefers-reduced-motion` calm version.
